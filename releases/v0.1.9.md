# v0.1.9

- Scroll past the last line of a document to keep your working area in the middle of the editor. This works by default, including in short files, without adding blank lines. Automatic centered scrolling remains optional in Settings → Editor.
- Restore readable guide layouts and expand the built-in instructions for editing, preview, exports, and presentations.
- Restore Windows builds and installer packaging with the MSYS2 UCRT64 GTK runtime.

Typst remains pinned to 0.15.1. Existing installations can use **Update available**, then **Restart to update**. Linux package installations update through their package manager.
