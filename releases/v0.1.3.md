# v0.1.3

Click equations in the preview to jump to their source, including parts that previously left the editor cursor in place.

- Fix clicks on integral strokes, radicals, descenders, generated differential symbols, and gaps within formulas.
- Use the formula's opening `$` when an individual glyph has no source target. Glyphs with a source target keep their precise cursor positions.
- Support equations in local imports and transformed or clipped content. Source mappings stay current when edits move lines without changing the preview.

Typst remains pinned to 0.15.1. Existing v0.1.2 installations can use **Update available**, then **Restart to update**. Linux package installations update through their package manager.
